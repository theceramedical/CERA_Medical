# UAT

A named CERA owner can do these without a developer:

- Confirm content changes go through a reviewed repository deployment; the local Payload CMS is not connected to production.
- Confirm a withdrawn service does not appear in the catalogue or accept an enquiry.
- Submit an enquiry, read the reference, and confirm the message is not in the customer confirmation.
- Confirm customer sign-in/account links are absent and old `/account/*` bookmarks return to Contact.
- Move an enquiry through a legal status change and confirm an illegal jump is rejected.
- Confirm an internal staff note is visible only in the staff workspace and does not enter an email/customer confirmation.
- Once prices/stock and the checkout gate are approved, submit a guest COD order and confirm staff see its exact lines and contact details.
