# UAT

A named CERA owner can do these without a developer:

- Publish a page in the CMS and see it on the public site after revalidation.
- Confirm a withdrawn service does not appear in the catalogue or accept an enquiry.
- Submit an enquiry, read the reference, and confirm the message is not in the customer confirmation.
- Sign in, claim an enquiry with the matching verified email, and see only that account's records.
- Move an enquiry through a legal status change and confirm an illegal jump is rejected.
- Confirm an internal note does not appear on the customer page or in the CRM payload.
