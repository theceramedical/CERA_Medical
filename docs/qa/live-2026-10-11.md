## Release-blocking findings

1. Vendure's live default channel is `USD`, while public catalogue prices were labeled `PKR`. A PKR 134,400 product appeared as US$134,400 in the cart. The preview said one item but `/cart` showed empty, because the API's bearer-token bridge was incompatible with a cookie-only Vendure configuration. The local fix enables both token methods, makes fresh cart reads authoritative, verifies persistence, and rejects non-PKR carts before checkout. The production channel and every price require reconciliation before purchasing can be reopened.
2. Seven physical SKUs, stock/quality/shipping specifications, and fixed prices came from unapproved prototype seed data. The local public API fix suppresses those exact SKUs, leaves records in Vendure for review, and makes any future physical listing enquiry-only. Five research services also had prototype fixed prices even though the approved brief requires scoped quotations; their public projection is now enquiry-only.
3. A test service (`university-of-swabi`) was public. The local API fix suppresses it from list and detail responses without deleting its record.
4. The published ribbon asserted lab accreditation and ISO compliance without supporting evidence. Local CMS bootstrap migration replaces only known legacy wording and preserves unrelated editor-written announcements. Other unverified claims in hardcoded About, Contact, Services and Products copy were removed locally.
5. Live legal pages still contain raw editorial instructions/placeholders from the client draft, and Terms of Service labels itself a draft. These pages need an exact editorial review and publication pass before legal completion can be claimed.
