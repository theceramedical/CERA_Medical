Frequently Asked Scoping & Governance Questions

Institutional guidelines governing research collaboration, biological transfer, and contractual compliance.

pets

How does CERA Medical handle ethics and animal welfare?

All in vivo studies strictly require Institutional Animal Ethics Committee (IAEC) approval prior to commencement. We operate in strict adherence to the internationally recognized 3Rs principles (Replacement, Reduction, and Refinement) and standard veterinary welfare parameters.

badge

Can external research sponsors visit the laboratory?

Yes. In-person facility audits, project oversight sessions, and physical specimen drop-offs are fully supported. In-person access requires protocol clearance and prior security notification for access to the PAF-IAST Haripur campus.

qr_code_2

What is the sample de-identification requirement?

We enforce a strict Zero Protected Health Information (Zero PII) policy. All human, clinical, or biological specimens must be pre-coded with alphanumeric 2D barcoded IDs before physical accession or digital database ingest.

request_quote

Do you provide formal quotes for grant applications?

Yes. We routinely generate institutional Letters of Support (LoS), formalized budgetary line-item schedules, and methodology descriptions for national and international grant submissions (e.g., HEC, NIH, Welcome Trust) within 5 working days.

Need Immediate Technical Clarification with a Study Director?

Our principal investigators and laboratory specialists are available for direct protocol feasibility assessments.
