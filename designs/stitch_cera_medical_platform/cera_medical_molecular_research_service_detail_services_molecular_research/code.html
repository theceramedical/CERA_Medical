Important Governance Notice & Research Scope

Research Scope & Human Sample Governance: Wet-lab services are conducted strictly for research and translational discovery, not direct clinical diagnosis. All human-derived specimens must be coded and supplied without direct participant identifiers, supported by Institutional Review Board (IRB) or Ethics Committee approval.

Wet-Lab Infrastructure & Analytical Rigor

photo_camera

CERA Molecular Research Suite — Haripur BSL-2 Facility. Equipped for high-fidelity Sanger sequencing, quantitative PCR, and multi-marker tissue immunohistochemistry.

CERA Medical provides comprehensive molecular, biochemical, and histological analysis designed to support academic principal investigators, biotechnology innovators, and pharmaceutical development programs. Operating within validated BSL-2 sterile laboratory suites, our technical team executes rigorous assays with defined positive/negative controls, multi-point standard curves, and documented replicate matrices.

All research workflows—from nucleic acid extraction through fluorescent microscopy and quantitative protein blotting—are conducted under strict Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs). Human-derived biospecimens must be coded prior to reception to ensure absolute participant anonymity and ethical alignment.

4-Tier Sample Governance & Chain-of-Custody

Rigorous tracking protocol to ensure absolute integrity from specimen intake to archiving.

1

Sample Reception & De-Identification Verification

Strict intake checking 2D cryogenic barcodes and verified lack of Personally Identifiable Information (PII). Un-coded clinical samples are rejected at intake.

2

Cold-Chain Integrity & Storage Monitoring

Continuous preservation via calibrated -80°C ultra-low temperature freezers and liquid nitrogen storage dewar banks equipped with redundant 24/7 cloud telemetry alarms.

3

Quality Control (QC) & Baseline Viability Gate

Fluorometric quantification, A260/A280 absorbance ratios, capillary RNA Integrity Number (RIN) scores, and total protein BCA validation prior to downstream assay allocation.

4

Post-Study Specimen Archival or Certified Destruction

Standard 90-day retention post-study technical dossier transmission, followed by certified biomedical autoclave incineration or pre-arranged secure cold-chain return shipment.

5-Stage Molecular Project Lifecycle

Standard operating timeline for scoping, assay execution, and publication-ready deliverable packaging.

STAGE 01

Scoping & Assay Matrix Agreement

Definition of experimental endpoints, replicates, biological power, volume tolerances, and limits of detection (LOD) documented in signed study charter.

STAGE 02

Ethics Clearance & Coded Sample Reception

Receipt of IRB/IEC approval documentation, transfer of anonymized 2D barcodes, and verification of cold-chain transit logs upon Haripur dock entry.

STAGE 03

Extraction, Reagent Verification & QC

Nucleic acid or protein lysis, batch lot traceability, negative extraction controls, and Qubit/NanoDrop yield verification before main run.

STAGE 04

Experimental Assay Execution

Blinded operator replicates, calibrated thermal cycler runs, automated liquid handling, and continuous digital raw data telemetry collection.

STAGE 05

Technical Dossier & Publication-Ready Figures

Delivery of normalized data matrices, standard curves, blot densitometry, raw .ab1 and .eds instrument files, and peer-review ready vector charts.
