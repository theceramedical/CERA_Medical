FACILITIES AND APPROACH

Laboratory Infrastructure & Computational Rigour

CERA Medical bridges bench wet-lab experiments with high-throughput in silico modeling. Our facility in Haripur integrates specialized biosafety suites with high-performance computing clusters.

Specialized Wet Laboratories

Dedicated sterile biosafety suites calibrated for primary cell assays, tissue preparation, and genetic analysis.

  • Animal House: Institutional ethical oversight
  • Cell Culture Lab: Glioblastoma & primary lines
  • Genomics Lab: Validated Sanger sequencing
  • Histopathology: Sectioning & fluorescent imaging

Computational Cluster

High-performance pipelines engineered for petabyte-scale metagenomic, transcriptomic, and deep clinical data modeling.

  • Nextflow & Snakemake: Reproducible workflows
  • Containerized: Docker/Singularity image lock
  • Versioned Repos: Full pipeline audits on Git
  • Air-Gapped: De-identified client storage vaults

Quality Assurance Protocol

Eliminating variance through standardized operational operating guidelines (SOPs) and documented handover chains.

  • Written Protocols: Method agreements prior to study
  • Clear Data Handover: Open formats + summary reports
  • Peer-Review Ready: Tables styled for target journals
